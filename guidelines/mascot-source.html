Canonical reference Mascot — source art

Hand-painted watercolour portrait by the artist. This is the single reference all cartoon and vector renditions of the mascot derive from; treat it as the master, not as a shippable asset.

  • Fixed traits. Wavy auburn hair with the forehead sweep, heavy dark brows, half-lidded eyes, warm pink cheek flush, teal open shirt over a white tee.
  • Do. Redraw from it — simplify shapes, flatten the palette, keep the proportions and expression recognisable.
  • Don't. Ship the painting itself in UI, crop it into an avatar, recolour it, or trace over the watercolour texture.
